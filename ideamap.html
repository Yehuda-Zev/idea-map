Drag on empty space to create your first box
Drag empty space: new box · Drag a box border or an arrow: new arrow (can end on boxes or arrows) · Wheel: zoom · Right/middle-drag or Space+drag: pan · Box selected: corner squares resize, corner button collapses (hold 3s = all) · Arrow selected: drag end dots to slide, Swap reverses · Double-click: rename title / arrow label · Del: delete
